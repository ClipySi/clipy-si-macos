# ClipySi Privacy Policy

**English** · [日本語](PRIVACY.ja.md)

_Last updated: 2026-06-28_

ClipySi is a clipboard manager for macOS. A clipboard manager sees everything you
copy — including passwords and tokens — so privacy is the first design constraint,
not an afterthought.

## Summary

- **Everything stays on your Mac.** Your clipboard history and snippets are stored
  locally and are not uploaded by ClipySi. (Cross-device sync is planned as an
  opt-in feature — see *Planned: optional cross-device sync* below.)
- **No telemetry by default.** Diagnostics are **off** out of the box and are
  opt-in. Even when enabled, they are collected locally and never sent
  automatically.
- **Your clipboard contents are never collected for diagnostics** — not the text,
  not titles, not snippet bodies, not your searches.
- **No ads, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.**

## Data ClipySi handles

| Data | Where it lives | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clipboard history (text, images, files you copy) | On your Mac only | Encrypted at rest (see below). |
| Snippets you create | On your Mac only | Stored in the local database. |
| Preferences | On your Mac only (`UserDefaults`) | e.g. history size, hotkeys, diagnostics level. |

ClipySi has no account, no login, and no server. It does not have a backend that
receives your data.

## Local storage & encryption

- Copied content (clip payloads and thumbnails) is encrypted at rest with CryptoKit
  **AES-GCM** and written as ciphertext on disk. The database holds **only
  metadata** — no copied content in plaintext.
- The encryption key is stored in the macOS **Keychain** with
  `kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`, so it is bound to your device
  and does not sync to iCloud.
- Pasteboard privacy markers (`org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType`, `TransientType`,
  `AutoGeneratedType`, and legacy synonyms) are honored **before any content is
  read**, so password-field and other concealed copies are never stored.
- Detected secrets (passwords, API keys, tokens) are **masked on-device** in the
  menu and history list. Detection runs entirely on your Mac — nothing is sent
  anywhere.

## What ClipySi never collects or sends

- Clipboard contents, images, or full file paths.
- Clip titles, snippet bodies, or search queries.
- Email addresses, OAuth tokens, encryption keys, or passphrases.
- The name or bundle identifier of the app you copied from.

## Diagnostics (opt-in, off by default)

To help fix crashes and bugs, ClipySi can collect a small amount of **anonymous,
on-device** diagnostic information. This is **off by default**. You choose a level
on first launch and can change it any time in **Settings → Diagnostics**.

Diagnostics are collected **only on your Mac**. ClipySi does **not** send them
anywhere automatically. If you want to share them with the developer, you can use
**Export Diagnostics…** to write a folder you can attach to a bug report. The
exported bundle is body-free by design (see below).

| Level | What is collected |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** (default) | Nothing — not even an installation identifier. |
| **Minimal** | Crash reports (via Apple's MetricKit), app and macOS version, CPU architecture, and an anonymous on-device installation ID. |
| **Standard** | Minimal, plus a coarse feature area on errors (e.g. `paste`, `menu`) and the sync type. |
| **Detailed** | Standard, plus recent coarse breadcrumbs — feature names only. |

The collected fields are limited by design: diagnostic events can only carry
predefined categories (a feature area, an error kind), never free-form text. There
is no code path that can attach clipboard content to a diagnostic, and an automated
test in the project enforces this on every change.

**Anonymous installation ID.** When you opt into Minimal or above, ClipySi
generates a random identifier on your Mac so repeated reports can be correlated.
It is not tied to you, your Apple Account, or any other identity, and it is never
created while diagnostics are Off.

**Apple's crash reporting.** macOS itself can deliver crash reports to a
developer through Apple's tools (Xcode Organizer) **only if you have opted in at
the operating-system level** (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics &
Improvements → *Share with App Developers*). Those reports are produced and
redacted by Apple and contain no clipboard content. This is an Apple feature
governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); ClipySi
does not control it.

## Software updates

When automatic update checks are enabled, ClipySi uses [Sparkle](https://sparkle-project.org)
to fetch an update feed (an "appcast") over the network. This request necessarily
reveals your IP address and a basic user-agent to the server hosting the feed, as
any web request does; it carries no clipboard data and no diagnostics. Automatic
checks are **on by default**, and you can turn them off (or check for updates
manually) at any time in the About window.

## Third-party services

ClipySi includes **no** advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party crash
reporters. The only network activity is the update check described above.

## Planned: optional cross-device sync

ClipySi is macOS-only today and, as described above, does not send your clipboard
data off your device. Cross-device sync is on the roadmap. If and when it ships, it
will be:

- **Opt-in, off by default** — nothing syncs unless you turn it on.
- **End-to-end encrypted** — encrypted on your device before it leaves, with a key
  derived from a passphrase that only you hold.
- **To storage you choose** — a local folder or your own cloud storage; ClipySi
  still has no server that receives your clipboard.

This policy will be updated with the specifics **before** any sync feature ships.

## Data retention & deletion

- Clipboard history is kept locally until you delete it (per item or "Clear
  History") or until it exceeds your configured maximum, which trims the oldest
  items. Snippets remain until you delete them.
- Stored crash reports and diagnostics remain on your Mac until you delete them.
- **Uninstalling** ClipySi and removing its data directory and Keychain item
  removes everything ClipySi stored. Because there is no server, there is nothing
  to request deletion of remotely.

## Children

ClipySi is a general-purpose developer/productivity tool and is not directed at
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the ClipySi
repository with a new "Last updated" date.
